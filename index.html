City Break

Name a few things you love - an artist, a film, a dish - and the agent queries the Qloo taste graph to find the short-break city that matches them, plus the venues inside it.

How this was built (agent tool trace) 

      

    

  


  

    Recommendations are generated from aggregate audience-affinity signals in the Qloo Insights API.
    Scores describe cultural affinity between tastes and places at audience level - they are not
    predictions about individuals, and venue lists reflect data coverage, not exhaustive directories.
    No personal data is stored by this app.
